# Offer: block arrival time dataset

b10c | 2025-10-13 17:23:34 UTC | #1

I maintain a public [bitcoin-data/block-arrival-times](https://github.com/bitcoin-data/block-arrival-times) dataset with timestamp of block arrival times collected across many different nodes.

Feel free to use the dataset - and if you happen to have timestamps that you can share, feel free to contribute them.
