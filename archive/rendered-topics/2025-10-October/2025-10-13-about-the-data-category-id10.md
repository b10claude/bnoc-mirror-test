# About the Data category

admin | 2025-10-13 17:08:18 UTC | #1

A place for sharing and requesting Bitcoin network data.

Feel free to "offer" data that you have and might want to share with others for research purposes. Be specific about e.g. dataset format, time range, collection methodology, ...

On the other side, feel free to "request" data here. Be precise about what you are looking for and what you want to do with the data.
